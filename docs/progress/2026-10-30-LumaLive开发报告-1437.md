先在PowerShell执行显式配置，再构建和测试，避免新增目标漏生成：

```powershell

  --parallel 1 -- /nr:false
& 'C:\Program Files\CMake\bin\ctest.exe' `
  --test-dir 'build/rtc-config-20261029' -C Release `
  -R '^luma_(preview_call|meeting_media|preview_rtc_config)_tests$' `
  --output-on-failure --timeout 90
$LASTEXITCODE
& 'C:\Python314\python.exe' -B -m unittest discover `
  -s scripts/windows -p test_account_thread_lifetimes.py -v
$LASTEXITCODE
```
